### MEMBERSHIP

We are excited you wish to join us!

Since 1932PASC has helped thousands of students, and their advisors, transform their schools and communities and growtheir leadership. Our organization exists to develop, engage, and celebrate leaders across our state. PASC helps students improve their lives while learning to lead through service, collaboration, and participation.
