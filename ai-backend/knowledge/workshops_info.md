# Workshop Information

## About Workshops
The conference features interactive workshops covering leadership skills, team building, communication, civic engagement, project planning, creativity, and student council best practices.

## Workshop Duration
Each workshop session is 30 minutes.

## Target Audience
Workshops are designed for student council members and can be tailored for students, advisors, or all attendees.

## Workshop Rotation System
The conference uses a rotation system with three groups (A, B, C) to ensure all attendees can experience multiple workshops:
- **Group A:** Workshop 1 → Lunch → Workshop 2
- **Group B:** Workshop 1 → Workshop 2 → Lunch
- **Group C:** Lunch → Workshop 1 → Workshop 2

Your rotation group will be assigned during registration.

## Workshop Topics
Workshops may cover topics such as:
- Leadership skills development
- Team building activities
- Effective communication
- Civic engagement and community service
- Project planning and execution
- Creative problem-solving
- Student council best practices
- Public speaking
- Goal setting

## Presenting a Workshop
Interested in presenting a workshop? Visit the Workshops page on the website to submit an application. Applications are submitted via Google Forms.

### Presenter Guidelines
- Sessions should be 30 minutes
- Interactive and engaging presentations are encouraged
- Topics should relate to leadership, student council, or personal development

## Workshop Rooms
Workshop rooms are named after planets in our solar system, fitting with the space theme.
