## Rotation Groups
Attendees are assigned to rotation groups (A, B, or C) during registration. This determines the order of workshops and lunch:
- **Group A:** Workshop 1 → Lunch → Workshop 2
- **Group B:** Workshop 1 → Workshop 2 → Lunch
- **Group C:** Lunch → Workshop 1 → Workshop 2
