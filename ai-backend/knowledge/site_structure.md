# Website Structure & Navigation

## Main Pages

### Home (/home)
The homepage features:
- Welcome message and conference announcement
- Theme display: "Reach for the Stars, Lead Beyond Limits"
- Latest announcements
- Quick info cards with key details
- Call-to-action buttons for registration and learning more

### About (/about)
Learn about PASC Region J:
- Organization history (founded 1932)
- Mission and vision statements
- Benefits of membership
- Activities and programs offered
- Service areas (Districts 11 and 12)

### Registration (/register)
Conference registration information:
- Registration window: January 5-23, 2026
- Who can register
- Registration process
- Contact for questions

### Workshops (/workshops)
Workshop information:
- Available workshop topics
- Workshop application for presenters
- Guidelines for presenters
- Session format (30 minutes each)

### Schedule (/schedule)
Conference timeline:
- Full day schedule from 10:30 AM to 4:00 PM
- Rotation group explanations
- Event descriptions
- Downloadable PDF schedule

### Gallery (/gallery)
Photo gallery:
- Photos from past conferences
- Browseable with pagination
- Lightbox viewing for full-size images

### Resources (/resources)
Conference resources:
- Downloadable documents and materials
- Conference brochures and flyers

## Floating Music Button (Available on ALL Pages)
The conference anthem "One Orbit" by IronRUST is accessible from ANY page on the website:
- Look for the orange music note button (🎵) in the bottom-left corner of the screen
- Click it to open the music player modal
- The button is ALWAYS visible on every page - no need to navigate to Resources!
- Features: play/pause, progress bar, download MP3, download instrumental version

### Contact (/contact)
Get in touch:
- Contact form for inquiries
- Email: info@pascregionj.com
- Subject options: General Inquiry, Conference Registration, Workshop Application, Other
- Response time: 24-48 hours during business days

## Quick Links
From any page, you can navigate to key sections using the main navigation menu.
