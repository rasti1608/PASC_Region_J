# Registration Information

## Registration Window
- **Opens:** January 5, 2026
- **Closes:** January 23, 2026

## Who Can Register
Student Council members from schools within PASC Region J (Districts 11 and 12) are eligible to register.

## Registration Process
Registration will be available through the website during the registration window. Check the Registration page on the website starting January 5, 2026.

## Cost
For specific pricing information, please contact info@pascregionj.com or check the Registration page when registration opens.

## Group Registration
Schools typically register their student council members as a group through their advisor.

## Questions About Registration
If you have questions about registration:
- Email: info@pascregionj.com
- Check the Registration page on the website
- Contact your school's Student Council advisor

## Important Notes
- Registration is only open during the registration window (January 5-23, 2026)
- Make sure to register before the deadline
- Late registrations may not be accommodated
